How to Turn Meeting Notes Into Action Items With AI

By Josh Rushlo · JBR Insights · Everyday Business AI · November 25, 2026

“We should update the website” can mean three different things when everyone leaves the room. Somebody agreed to do it, everybody agreed it needs doing, or one person floated the idea. Ask AI for a meeting summary and all three can sound equally settled. Ask it to show who actually agreed to what, and you get something the team can use.

You can do this with handwritten notes you type up afterward. Short fragments are fine. Keep people’s names beside their own words, especially when someone says “I’ll take that.” For a first try, use ordinary office business and leave sensitive personnel or customer details out.

From a messy office huddle to three action items

Here are fictional notes from a September 8, 2026, huddle at a business with a customer pickup counter.

Agreed to try pickup until 6 p.m. Wednesday Sept 16. One night only. Nora said, “I’ll replace the lobby sign by noon Friday Sept 11.” Eli said, “I’ll check whether the cleaner can come at 6:15 p.m. that night.” Pickup page online still says 5. Needs updating by Sept 15, nobody took it. Sam thinks we ought to stay open every Wednesday if this works. Left that for later.

What to ask the AI

Turn the notes below into a recap. Separate decisions we made, actions people need to take, and ideas still open. For each action, give me the task, owner, and deadline. Use “unknown” for a missing owner or deadline. Keep the dates accurate and make clear what each time refers to. Give me the questions that still need an answer before I send the recap.

What the finished recap should look like

Decision made. Trial customer pickup until 6 p.m. on Wednesday, September 16, for one night.

Actions from the September 8 huddle
ActionOwnerDeadline
Replace the lobby sign with the trial pickup hours.NoraNoon Friday, Sept 11
Ask whether the cleaner can come at 6:15 p.m. on Sept 16.EliUnknown
Update the pickup page for the one-night trial.UnknownSept 15

Idea still open. Sam suggested extended hours every Wednesday if the trial works. The team hasn’t agreed to a recurring schedule.

Check the gaps before you send it

Two questions for the team will finish this recap. Who has access to update the pickup page and will take it by September 15? When will Eli get back to the team with the cleaner’s answer? The 6:15 time belongs to the proposed cleaning visit. Putting that time in Eli’s deadline column could leave everyone waiting until the night of the trial.

Read the recap against the notes, fill in what the team confirms, and then circulate it. At the next huddle, you can open with whether the sign went up, the website changed, and the cleaner agreed to the time.
