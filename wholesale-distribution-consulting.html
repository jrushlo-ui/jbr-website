Before We Talk

Questions About Working Together

What does a fractional sales leader do for a distributor?

A fractional sales leader provides part-time sales management and direction. At JBR, the scope can include team meetings, account and territory planning, opportunity reviews, coaching, and coordination with operations.

Can you coach our current sales manager?

Yes. The engagement can support the manager you already have through account reviews, coaching, clearer team expectations, and a consistent management rhythm.

How does fractional leadership compare with a full-time sales director?

Fractional support can suit a defined leadership gap or a business that needs part-time management capacity. A full-time role may suit a larger, ongoing workload. Read the fractional versus full-time sales leadership comparison for the trade-offs.

Do we need a new CRM before working together?

No. We first look at the way sales operates and the information available. Improvements can begin with your existing tools; any technology changes should follow the needs of the team.
