Before We Talk

Questions About Working Together

Do you start with a CRM replacement?

No. JBR starts with how sales work moves through the business. We examine responsibilities, information, and follow-through before deciding whether a tool needs to change.

Can you help improve quote follow-up?

Yes. A focused process review can examine how quotes are assigned, how next steps are agreed with customers, and how stalled opportunities reach a manager’s attention.
