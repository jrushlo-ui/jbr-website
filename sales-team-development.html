Before We Talk

Questions About Working Together

Is this a one-time sales training workshop?

JBR focuses on coaching around the accounts and territories your team manages. The scope can be a focused project or ongoing coaching, with expectations and follow-up agreed before the work begins.

Can you work with inside and outside sales teams?

Yes. The work can cover account ownership, communication, opportunity handoffs, and coaching across inside and outside sales roles.
