How to Build a Distributor Sales Channel

JBR Insights · Channel Strategy

A strong distributor sales channel can create leverage, expand market reach, and make growth more repeatable. But simply signing distributors does not create a productive channel. The channel has to operate as a commercial system.

Define What Success Looks Like

Clarify target markets, customer segments, geographic coverage, product fit, commercial expectations, and the support partners will need to succeed.

Choose Partners for Fit, Not Just Reach

Consider customer alignment, sales capability, reputation, complementary products, field coverage, and willingness to actively develop the business.

Establish Clear Roles

Define account ownership, territory expectations, lead handling, pricing communication, customer support, and how direct sales interacts with the channel.

Build a Repeatable Operating Rhythm

Use regular communication, opportunity reviews, market feedback, joint planning, and clear expectations to keep the relationship productive.

A distributor is an account. A productive distributor network is a commercial system.
