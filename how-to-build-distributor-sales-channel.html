How to Build a Distributor Sales Channel

By Josh Rushlo · JBR Insights · Channel Strategy · Published September 19, 2026

A strong distributor channel can create leverage and expand market reach. But signing a distributor is only the beginning. A distributor is an account. A productive distributor network is a commercial system.

Define What Success Looks Like

Start with the market rather than a list of potential partners. Clarify the customers you want to reach, geographic gaps, product fit, service expectations, margin structure, and what a productive partner should actually contribute.

Choose Partners for Fit, Not Just Reach

A large distributor is not automatically the right distributor. Look at customer alignment, field coverage, complementary products, sales capability, reputation, willingness to develop the business, and whether your offering matters enough to earn attention.

Establish Clear Roles

Define account ownership, territory expectations, lead handling, pricing communication, customer support, and how direct sales interacts with the channel. Ambiguity creates channel conflict faster than almost anything else.

Build a Repeatable Operating Rhythm

Productive partnerships need communication, opportunity reviews, market feedback, joint planning, and clear expectations. If the only contact is a quarterly check-in asking why sales are down, there is no real channel-management system.

A distributor is an account. A productive distributor network is a commercial system.
