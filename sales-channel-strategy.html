Before We Talk

Questions About Working Together

Can you help with existing distributors as well as new ones?

Yes. An underperforming network is often the right place to start. JBR can review current partners and help improve expectations, support, and account activity. New partner development follows the market gaps the review identifies.

What is included in a distributor performance review?

The scope can include sales and reorder trends, customer coverage, partner engagement, lead handling, product support, and shared account plans. We agree on the available information and the decisions the review needs to support before starting.

Do you work with our existing sales team?

Yes. Josh works alongside leadership and the people responsible for the channel. Support can include partner reviews, coaching, joint planning, and implementation with your team.

Can you help manage direct sales and distributor conflict?

Yes. The work can address account ownership, lead handling, partner expectations, and rules for direct and indirect sales. Read the guide to managing channel conflict for the issues to examine.
