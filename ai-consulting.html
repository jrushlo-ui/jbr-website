A Practical Engagement

Find the Opportunity. Test It. Make It Useful.

1. Understand the workflow

Discuss your goals, current tools, bottlenecks, and the work your team repeats. Identify where better processes or AI support could help.

2. Choose a focused starting point

Prioritize a practical use case, agree on a scope, and define what to measure—such as time spent, handoff completeness, or follow-up consistency. See how a distributor chooses its first sales workflow to automate.

3. Build, test, and refine

Develop a practical workflow, test it with the people doing the work, and improve it based on what they learn. The scope may include templates, tool setup, training, or implementation support.

4. Keep people in control

Establish review steps and consider data sensitivity, access, and tool settings before putting business or customer information into an AI system. Verify important outputs before acting on them.

Common Questions

Questions About AI Consulting

Do we need to replace our CRM or existing software?

Not necessarily. The starting point is your business process and the tools you already use. Any changes depend on the use case, your systems, and the scope you choose.

Does our team need technical experience?

No. We can start with everyday tasks and straightforward examples, then work at a pace that fits your people and business.

Can AI send customer messages automatically?

Some tools support automation, but a suitable workflow depends on your systems and business requirements. A sensible starting point is to draft messages for a person to review and send.

How do we get started?

Tell Josh where work is getting stuck or which repetitive tasks you would like to improve. You can discuss a focused assessment, implementation project, or ongoing support.
